# Combat

Combat begins when an individual or group chooses violence. When combat is initiated, the passage of time is measured in rounds. A round is about one minute of time. Combat is broken down in three steps: **Surprise/Initiative, Move, and Action.**

### Surprise/Initiative: 
On the first round of combat, before initiative is rolled, if there is a reasonable chance of one side or the other being surprised, each of the opposing groups rolls `1d6`. On a 1-2, that group is
**surprised**. The surprised group takes no actions in the first round. At the beginning of a round, each side rolls `1d6`. The side that **rolled higher acts first**. Ties result in **simultaneous actions**.
