# Select a Class
Three classes are available: Cleric, Fighter, and Magic-User. Each class has an advancement table that details its specifics. 
These include:
- **Level**: This is a general measure of a character’s skill. It starts at 1.
- **XP**: This is the number of Experience Points required to earn the listed level.
- **HD**: Hit Dice. At character creation and each time a PC gains a level, they roll this total to determine their maximum Hit Points. A
PC’s Constitution modifier is applied to each die rolled, but the total always goes up by at least +1 each time a level is gained.
- **AB**: A PC’s Attack Bonus. It is added to all attack rolls.
- **ST**: Saving Throw. When faced with a danger or threat, a PC might be asked to roll a d20. If the result, after modifiers, is equal to or
over this number, the threat is mitigated or avoided entirely.
