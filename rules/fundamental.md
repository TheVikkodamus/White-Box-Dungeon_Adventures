## Fundamental Concepts
This game assumes familiarity with fundamental vintage roleplaying
game concepts. Readers are assumed to understand what an RPG is,
shorthand notation for dice, and terms like “dungeon crawl.” If the reader
is unfamiliar with the fundamental concepts of roleplaying games, a quick
search online can give a more detailed primer than this book ever could.

## Back to the Dungeon
White Box: Dungeon Adventures does not provide detailed information
on hex crawls, developing campaign settings, wilderness encounters, or
other information commonly found in other vintage style RPGs. It focuses
solely on dungeon exploration and assumes that Referees who wish to
expand beyond the dungeon can integrate material from White Box
Cyclopedia or countless other vintage style adventure games.

## Referee Fiat and Internal Logic
There are fewer concrete rules in this game than in many other RPGs -
even other White Box-based RPGs. Player characters should be proactive,
ask questions, investigate, and explore their environment. They should
not rely on their character sheet and game rules, but on their own intellect
to solve problems. The Referee will, in turn, adjudicate the results of
observations and actions in an unbiased manner.
