# Monsters and Treasure

## Magic Item

Monsters usually carry `1d6` gold pieces in treasure per Hit Die. To determine if a monster has any magic items, roll `1d20` per HD of the Monster. If any number rolled is equal to or less than the monster’s HD, then roll 
on Table 6-1: Magic Items to determine a magic item carried by the monster. Some monsters hoard no treasure (like giant centipedes), while others hoard a great amount of treasure (like dragons). The Referee can modify the amount
of treasure found based on logic and narrative consistency.

### Table 6-1: Magic Items
|||||
|:---:|:---|:---:|:---|
|1-3| Armor|12| Staff|
|4-7| Potion|13-15| Wand|
|8| Ring|16-18|Weapon|
|9| Scroll|19-20|Misc. Item|

## Armor

Magical armor and magical shields have a listed bonus that is added to the wearer’s Armor Class.

### Table 6-2: Magic Armor
|||||
|:---:|:---|:---:|:---|
|1-4|Chainmail +1|13-14|Platemail +1|
|5-6|Chainmail +2|15| Platemail +2|
|7-10|Leather +1|16-20|Shield +1|
|11-12|Leather +2|||

## Potion 

Potions can be drunk by anyone. Unless noted, their effects last one hour. Roll `1d6` to determine the item found.
