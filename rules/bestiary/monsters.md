# Monsters

Monsters populate dungeons, which serve as lairs and hunting grounds for the foul creatures of the underworld.

## Monster Stats
Monsters have a simplified stat block. Unless otherwise noted, monsters function like PCs. Special abilities are noted in monster descriptions. All monster attacks inflict `1d6` damage unless otherwise noted.

- **Armor Class (AC)**: Functions as described on page 14.
- **Hit Dice (HD)**: This is the number of d6s rolled to determine a monster’s Hit Points. Monsters add +1 to their Attack Rolls for each Hit Die.
- **Saving Throw (SV)**: Functions as described on page 8.
- **Experience Points (XP)**: The amount of XP the PCs earn for defeating the monster.

Unless otherwise noted, non-human monsters can see in the dark. Monsters between 1-2 HD appear in groups of `2d6`. Monsters between `3-4` HD appear in groups of `1d6`. Monsters with 5+ HD are almost always
alone.
